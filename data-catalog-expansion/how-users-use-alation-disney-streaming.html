How people use Alation
at Disney Streaming

A short, readable guide to what the catalog is for, what most people do in it every week, and how different roles rely on it—without drowning in product jargon.

In one sentence: Alation is the place to find data, understand what it means, see how it flows, and align on business definitions—so teams spend less time asking around and more time using data with confidence.

What it is

Alation is the company’s data catalog. Instead of guessing from cryptic table names or pinging the one person who “knows that schema,” people open the catalog to discover datasets, read descriptions and ownership, and check how pieces connect.

At Disney Streaming, Alation has been the primary catalog for Data Governance (business glossary, stewardship, discovery, and lineage). The organization is also moving toward an internal catalog (OMS); this guide describes how people use Alation today.

What most people do

These are the everyday moves—search, browse, read, and trust.

Search

Type a topic (for example “customer” or “subscription”). Open matching tables, columns, articles, or glossary terms. Use filters by object type when results are broad.

Discovery

Browse the hierarchy

When you don’t know the exact name, go data source → schema → table → column—like folders for Snowflake and other connected systems.

Navigation

Read the asset page

On a table or column, read descriptions, owners and stewards, tags, and custom fields so you know what it is and whether it’s the right asset to use.

Context

Use the business glossary

Look up official definitions of metrics and terms. Many terms link to the data or queries that implement that definition.

Shared language

Follow lineage

See where data comes from and where it goes—upstream and downstream. Helps with trust, debugging, and impact (“if this changes, what breaks?”).

Flow & impact

Read and write docs

Articles hold longer guidance: onboarding, how a domain works, stewardship processes—not just one-line descriptions.

Knowledge base

Queries (power users)

Compose is the SQL workspace: write, save, and share queries. Saved queries can appear in the catalog and feed lineage.

Analysts & stewards

Tags & signals

Tags classify assets (domain, sensitivity, and more). Governance signals help others filter quickly and see risk or trust at a glance.

Classification

By role

Same tool—different jobs to be done.

Who Typical use of Alation
Analysts & BI Search and browse to find data; read documentation and lineage; use saved queries; ask questions or leave comments on assets.
Data engineers Lineage and technical metadata for pipelines and impact analysis; understanding how sources and jobs connect; APIs where used.
Data stewards & owners Keep glossary terms, descriptions, tags, and ownership accurate; publish articles; curate their domain.
Governance & compliance Tags, policies, glossary, and lineage for standards and risk; exports and reports where configured.
Leadership Less day-to-day clicking in the catalog; more adoption and usage visibility where those dashboards or reports exist.

How you get in

People usually sign in with SSO (single sign-on), like other enterprise apps. Who can see which data sources depends on roles and permissions in Alation and your identity setup.
