What the Data Governance Portal is — and what it does for us

Slide 1 of 3

Definitions

Data Governance Portal — The privacy and data governance command center in the shared OMS shell: one place for posture, exceptions, and evidence across guest and consumer data — organized by functional dimensions, not org titles.

Unified Data Governance Experience (UDGE) — The broader program story: a single shell (Data Navigator & Governance Portal) that connects the Portal (govern & prove) with Business Data Navigator (discover & steward catalog assets) without collapsing specialist backends into one screen.

Four dimensions — Privacy (consent, DSRs, tracker remediation) · Platform (stewards, classifications, policies) · Security (access, GIS, data use) · Regulatory (laws & regulations, incidents, audits).

Design principle: Unified experience, federated execution — one portal for privacy, platform, security, and regulatory posture; specialist systems (MAUI, consent platform, Kronos RIM, Navigator Requests) remain authoritative for deep workflow.

What it does for the organization

  • Governance Overview — Top-line Privacy Compliance Index (PCI) plus Platform, Security, and Regulatory section indices — executives see “are we in policy?” in one glance.
  • Dimension hubs — Purpose-built views for Privacy, Platform, Security, and Regulatory — each with KPIs, trend charts, Overview + Work queue tabs, and the top items needing attention.
  • Per-function work queues — Unified queue model filtered by dimension — DSR SLA breaches, tracker findings, classification sign-offs, data-use intake — with handoff to the right tool.
  • Defined vs observed — Side-by-side policy intent and operational reality on shared catalog assets — definitions, classifications, and what OMS actually sees.
  • Evidence & auditor view — Export evidence packages and open auditor-oriented summaries from the Portal header for regulatory and internal audit requests.
  • Navigator spine links — Platform dimension links stewards to Navigator (Data Sources, Catalog); Classifications and Policies stay in Portal with deep ties to catalog assets.
  • Reduce dashboard sprawl — One front door instead of five disconnected privacy/compliance URLs with no shared story.

Architecture: Governance Portal lands first for privacy & compliance stakeholders; Business Data Navigator remains the action layer for stewards and analysts. Same OMS metadata underneath — two modes in one shell, one truth.

Why it matters — stakeholders, risk, and the wider org

Slide 2 of 3

Why it matters now

  • Fragmented dashboards — Each workstream (consent, DSRs, trackers, access, GIS, data use) has its own tool; stakeholders cannot see overall posture or prioritize across dimensions.
  • Privacy stakeholder mandate — Leadership asked for a holistic, Abacus-inspired single unified experience for Privacy, Legal, Compliance, GIS, and technical data owners — without rebuilding every backend.
  • Action vs reporting gap — Posture charts alone do not answer “what needs a human this week?” — the Portal must triage and route, not only report.
  • Catalog migration context — As Alation exits (Oct 1, 2026), governance and privacy teams need a stable home that links to OMS-backed catalog assets — not another silo.

Data Governance benefits

  • Persona-appropriate defaults — Privacy/legal land on Portal overview; DPMs/engineers complete work in Navigator Requests with Portal visibility for SLA breaches.
  • Functional IA — Dimensions grouped by job (Privacy, Platform, Security, Regulatory) — not by internal org names like “Operations” or legacy “Compliance.”
  • Audit trail narrative — Exceptions and approvals tied to catalog objects support explainable data use and regulatory inquiry.
  • Program alignment — Connects FY27 privacy & governance priorities to operational metrics teams already track downstream.

Benefits for the whole org

  • Privacy, legal & GIS — One map of consent posture, DSR volume, tracker remediation, and GIS security compliance — with drill-down to MAUI-style operational views where needed.
  • Security & compliance — Access reviews, GIS controls, and data-use approvals visible in the Security dimension — linked to downstream systems (including Kronos RIM where records management execution lives).
  • Platform stewards & DPMs — Stewardship coverage, classification approvals, and policy mappings surfaced in Platform queues with full asset context in Navigator.
  • Business & analytics consumers — Indirect benefit: faster resolution of governance blockers on datasets they depend on; clearer trust signals on catalog assets.
  • Executives & program leads — Governance Overview: PCI-style index, Platform / Security / Regulatory section scores, open exceptions by severity, and exportable evidence for council reviews.
  • Engineering & platform — Fewer “which dashboard?” questions; clear handoffs from Portal triage to Requests / Workflows in Navigator.
FY27 framing: The Governance Portal is the operating layer for privacy, platform, security, and regulatory posture — not a replacement for DSR, consent, or catalog tools, but the shell that makes the whole program legible to non-specialists.

Value now — value at scale — and how we measure success

Slide 3 of 3

Foundation & near-term

  • Portal shell & IA — Four dimension buckets (Privacy, Platform, Security, Regulatory) with Governance Overview as default landing — validated in UDGE demo.
  • Workstream prototypes — Consent, DSRs, tracker, stewardship, classifications, policies, access, GIS, data use, laws, incidents, and audits with KPI cards and representative charts.
  • Work queue model — Overview + Work queue tabs per function; golden-path seeds with Navigator / governance handoffs for classification and metadata approvals.
  • Navigator boundary — Clear split: Portal for posture & triage; Navigator for catalog discovery and steward execution.
  • Stakeholder reviews — Privacy, legal, and program leads refine container layout and metrics (Suman: “containers/format first; metric details iterate”).

Value at scale

  • Live data federation — KPIs and queue items fed from consent, DSR, tracker, access, GIS, and data-use systems — not static demo numbers.
  • Role-based defaults — Privacy/compliance default to Governance Overview; stewards default to Navigator Requests; executives to overview + evidence export.
  • Evidence automation — Scheduled evidence packages and auditor views tied to catalog asset IDs and policy mappings.
  • OMS integration — Classifications, policies, and stewardship state read from operational metadata store — defined vs observed stays current.
  • Enterprise expansion — Additional segments and domains inherit dimension model; shared PCI and exception taxonomy across BUs where policy aligns.

How we measure success

  • Posture indices — Privacy Compliance Index (PCI) and Platform / Security / Regulatory section indices trended month-over-month with target thresholds.
  • Exception SLA — % of work-queue items closed within SLA; count of breaches by dimension.
  • Portal adoption — Weekly active users among privacy, legal, compliance, and stewardship roles.
  • Handoff completion — % of Portal-routed items actioned in Navigator / downstream tools within agreed windows.
  • Evidence readiness — Time to produce audit / regulatory evidence package from Portal export.
  • Stakeholder satisfaction — Qualitative feedback from privacy council and intake forums on “single unified experience” vs prior sprawl.
Phasing: IA + demo containers → live feeds + queue → evidence automation + OMS integration → enterprise scale alongside Navigator catalog migration.
