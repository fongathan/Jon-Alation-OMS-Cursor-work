Data Governance Portal

Program: Unified Data Governance Experience (UDGE)  |  Program lead: Fong, Jonathan  |  Key partners: Privacy, Legal, GIS, Compliance, OMS, Business Data Navigator, Kronos RIM

What

The Governance Portal is the privacy and data governance command center in the shared OMS shell—one front door for posture, exceptions, and evidence across guest and consumer data. One portal for privacy, platform, security, and regulatory posture, organized by four dimensions: Privacy, Platform, Security, and Regulatory.

Why

Each workstream already has its own dashboard; stakeholders cannot see overall posture or prioritize across dimensions. Leadership asked for a single unified experience (Abacus-inspired) without rebuilding every backend—unified experience, federated execution.

Stakeholders

Privacy, legal, compliance, and GIS teams; data stewards and DPMs; executives and program leads; internal audit; business and analytics consumers (indirect—faster resolution of governance blockers on datasets they use).

Top things to know

  • Portal vs Navigator: Portal = posture, triage, evidence; Navigator = catalog discovery and steward execution (Data Sources, Catalog, Requests, Workflows).
  • Dimension IA: Privacy (Consent, DSRs, Tracker) · Platform (Stewards → Navigator, Classifications, Policies) · Security (Access, GIS, Data Use) · Regulatory (Laws, Incidents, Audits).
  • Phase: Governance hub is phase 2 alongside Navigator MVP for catalog migration (Alation exit Oct 1, 2026).
  • Not in scope: Replacing DSR, consent, tracker, or downstream RIM consoles—deep links and per-function work queues instead.

OKRs & metrics (draft)

  • Privacy Compliance Index — Top-line PCI plus Platform, Security, and Regulatory section indices trended with target thresholds.
  • Exception SLA — % of work-queue items closed within SLA; breaches by dimension (Privacy, Platform, Security, Regulatory).
  • Portal adoption — Weekly active users among privacy, legal, compliance, and stewardship roles.
  • Handoff completion — % of Portal-routed items actioned in Navigator or downstream tools on time.
  • Evidence readiness — Time to produce audit / regulatory evidence package from Portal export.

Timing / milestones (indicative)

  • [Now] UDGE demo: Privacy / Platform / Security / Regulatory IA, Governance Overview (PCI + section indices), work queues, auditor view & evidence export.
  • [Phase 2 start] Stakeholder sign-off on containers/format; live KPI feeds from dimension workstreams.
  • [With catalog migration] Defined-vs-observed on OMS assets; Navigator Requests handoffs wired.
  • [Scale] Evidence automation, role-based defaults, enterprise segment expansion.
