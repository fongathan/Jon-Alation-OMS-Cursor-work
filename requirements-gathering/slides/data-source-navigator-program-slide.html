Data Navigator

Program lead: Fong, Jonathan  |  Governance / intake: Data Platforms Intake (Bi-weekly)  |  Key partners: OMS, CPL / foundations, data governance (classification), Data 626 / MCI

What

Data Navigator is the governed discovery experience on OMS—the new home for business metadata, glossary, and metric definitions where ~531 Alation users search, browse, and trust assets after migration. Navigator is the UX layer, not the metadata engine: automated capture, reconciliation, and AI-enriched definitions are produced by Data 626 / MCI and OMS; Navigator is where that work surfaces for business and steward personas.

Why

Alation is a passive catalog (~531 users) that drifts from production and cannot carry us past contract renewal. We need an in-house surface on OMS that preserves discovery workflows, migrates Alation content, and composes trusted metadata for the right personas—while upstream programs improve metadata supply without duplicating scrapers in this L3.

Stakeholders

Business & DNA consumers and analysts (primary); data stewards and privacy/compliance reviewers (secondary); OMS platform; Data 626 / MCI (metadata supply); DNA / Metrics Council (definitions alignment); enterprise design (Dave Hoffman); metadata working group; leadership sponsors for intake and renewal planning.

Top things to know

  • Boundary: Navigator ≠ scrapers, collectors, or MCP/agentic access—those sit in MCI, Data 626, OMS backend; Navigator surfaces outcomes via OMS APIs.
  • MVP: Search/discovery, glossary, browse, descriptions/metadata, APIs to migrate Alation; persona/filter UX is exploratory (prototype & A/B, not frozen wireframes).
  • Adjacent: Governance Portal is phase 2 / tangential—privacy/compliance command center, not in this L3 scope.
  • Renewal: Alation renewal likely Aug/Sep 2026; engineering targets Sep 2027 cutover (dev complete ~Mar 2027, migration window before renewal).
