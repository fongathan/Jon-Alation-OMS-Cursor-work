Can Unity Catalog replace Alation?

Slide 1 of 8

The question

After Databricks Data + AI Summit 2026 (June 15–18), leadership is asking whether Unity Catalog can substitute for building an in-house catalog on OMS—instead of our Alation migration program.

Answer: No — UC complements OMS; it does not replace Alation.

Business glossary: the decisive gap

Slide 6 of 8

What stewards need (F02)

  • Terms with definitions, synonyms, status
  • Links to tables & columns
  • Domain & stewardship ownership
  • Searchable by business language

Example Alation terms: Paid Subscribers, Total Time Streamed per Entitled Account

What UC offers instead

  • Metric Views — SQL-defined KPIs with display names & synonyms on measures/dimensions
  • Agent metadata — helps Genie/AI interpret metrics
  • Future Glossary — announced DAIS 2026; preview coming soon
These are not interchangeable. A metrics layer standardizes how KPIs are calculated. A business glossary standardizes what concepts mean across the organization—including terms that are not single SQL metrics.

Industry view (Architect Mindset): UC = structural engine; enterprise catalog = collaboration & federation layer.

Recommended architecture — complement, don’t substitute

Slide 7 of 8
Unity Catalog Execution-plane governance for Databricks: schemas, permissions, lineage, metric views.
626 / MCI Technical metadata supply into OMS.
OMS Federated metadata store — system of record for enterprise catalog (technical + business).

Ingest UC metadata via APIs/crawlers. One inventory across Snowflake, Delta, pipelines, BI.

Navigator Business-first UX — Alation replacement: search, glossary, asset detail, stewardship.

Principle: one system of record per metadata type — no dual-writing glossary in UC and OMS.

Decision & asks

Slide 8 of 8

Decision

Do not pivot Alation replacement to Unity Catalog.

Do continue OMS + Navigator; integrate UC as a governed Databricks metadata source.

Do not pause for UC

Discover/Glossary previews and Governance Hub are not ready for Alation exit. Contract ends Oct 1, 2026.

Talking points for forums

  • “Can UC replace Alation?” — Not for glossary, cross-platform discovery, or stewardship across our estate.
  • “Are we duplicating Databricks?” — UC governs the lakehouse; OMS federates; Navigator is the experience business users need.
  • “What did DAIS change?” — Better metrics, domains, and agent governance on Databricks; still not an enterprise glossary or cross-platform catalog.

Asks

  • Align on OMS + Navigator as Alation replacement strategy
  • Charter UC → OMS integration for Databricks assets & metric views
  • Track UC Glossary GA for future OMS ingestion—not a program reset
