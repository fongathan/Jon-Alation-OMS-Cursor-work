What Data Navigator is — and what it does for us

Slide 1 of 3

Definitions

Data Navigator — The governed discovery experience on OMS: search, browse, glossary, classifications (read), gold/provenance, and steward-facing workflows. It is where business metadata and metric definitions live for business and analyst personas—and the target home for Alation content migrated via APIs.

Not Navigator — Scrapers, collectors, MCP/agentic catalog access, and automated metadata reconciliation. Those sit in Data 626 / MCI, OMS backend, and the metadata working group. Navigator surfaces upstream outcomes; it does not perform the AI anchoring itself.

Alation (transitioning out) — Current vendor catalog for ~531 users: glossary, articles, stewardship, search, lineage views. Contract end Oct 1, 2026; renewal likely Aug/Sep 2026 while we build toward Sep 2027 cutover.

Stack: 626 / MCI → metadata supply · OMS → store · Navigator → governed UX. One spine; Navigator is the experience layer only.

What it does for the organization

  • Find and trust governed metadata — Business and DNA consumers search and browse with owner, classification, and provenance visible—without tribal knowledge or ad hoc Slack threads.
  • Home for definitions — Glossary-linked business meaning and corporate metric definitions (aligned with DNA / Metrics Council) composed from OMS—not a passive wiki that drifts.
  • Surface upstream AI & automation — When MCI / Data 626 produce enriched descriptions, lineage, or suggested mappings, Navigator is where stewards and consumers see and act on that work.
  • Migrate Alation — APIs and UX paths to move glossary, articles, endorsements, and discovery workflows into the in-house stack without losing steward habits.
  • Persona-first defaults — Primary: business/DNA consumer & analyst. Secondary: thin steward workflows; privacy/compliance read views. Engineers deep-link to OMS UI.
  • Enterprise UX — Governed by enterprise design (Dave Hoffman / Sage-style patterns); wireframes are exploratory—iterate with users in the loop.
  • Read API for integrators — DRE and downstream tools consume governed metadata from OMS; Navigator is not the only channel.

Boundary: Governance Portal (privacy/compliance command center) is phase 2 / adjacent—not in Navigator MVP scope.

Why it matters — migration, personas, and program boundaries

Slide 2 of 3

Why it matters now

  • Alation exit pressure — ~531 users depend on a vendor catalog that cannot be replaced in 3–4 months; we need an honest renewal + build narrative (Aug 2026 renewal; Sep 2027 cutover target).
  • Passive catalog drift — Manual curation decouples from production; Navigator on OMS gives a durable home as technical metadata strengthens underneath.
  • Competing-program confusion — Intake asked for crisp boundaries: 626 improves supply, OMS stores, Navigator composes the governed experience—no duplicate scrapers in this L3.
  • Users in the loop — Romit/Suman/Tony: involve stewards, business users, privacy, and DNA during build—not a six-month surprise reveal.

Data Governance benefits

  • Single vocabulary — Metric and glossary definitions governed in one place; DNA / Metrics Council handover to GIS stays aligned.
  • Stewardship continuity — Migrate Alation workflows; federated ownership and attestation visible in Navigator thin paths.
  • Metadata working group — Shared picture of how Navigator slots vs. MCI, Data 626, and classification—fewer duplicate bets.
  • AI exposure elsewhere — Tony’s MCP/agentic patterns belong in MCI / Data 626; Navigator stays a traditional UI that surfaces trusted outputs.

Benefits for the whole org

  • Business & DNA consumers — Authoritative metric definitions and dataset discovery; answers “which number is official?” without engineering escalation.
  • Analysts & BI — Shared browse/search spine with gold definitions and known conflicts surfaced at discovery time.
  • Stewards & DPMs — Endorse, reconcile, and route metadata work; upstream automation lands in a UI they already use for discovery.
  • Privacy & compliance — Read-oriented, role-filtered views; Governance Portal handles posture/triage in phase 2.
  • Engineering & platform — OMS remains technical SoR; fewer “which tool?” questions; stable read APIs for automation.
  • Leadership & procurement — Credible migration plan supporting renewal flexibility and Sep 2027 exit with dev complete ~Mar 2027.
Intake framing (28 May): Navigator is the bridge from Alation to in-house OMS—MVP UX + migration APIs, persona-first, enterprise design–governed—not a metadata engine or governance portal rebuild.

Value now — value at scale — and how we measure success

Slide 3 of 3

Foundation & near-term

  • Intake & scope lock — Document MVP vs. out-of-scope (UI migration vs. MCI/Data 626 vs. MCP); complete RICE in Airtable.
  • Persona stories — Five stories each for business consumer, analyst, steward; validate with Sarah/Suman/privacy/DNA.
  • Exploratory wireframes — Persona/filter UX (filters vs. access-based vs. dual entry)—prototype & A/B when greenlit.
  • MVP in scope — Search/browse, gold/provenance, glossary, classifications read, read API, Open in OMS, Alation migration APIs.
  • MVP out of scope — DSAR/tracker/consent consoles, DRE remediation UI, governance queue, MCP/agentic access, SQL/social admin.

Value at scale

  • Full Alation decommission — Users migrated; glossary, articles, and discovery parity on Navigator/OMS (Sep 2027 target).
  • Richer upstream feeds — As 626/MCI improve metadata supply, Navigator shows fresher definitions, lineage context, and AI-suggested updates without owning the engines.
  • Semantic / metrics integration — Corporate metric definitions federated; catalog linked to platform-agnostic semantic layer where policy aligns.
  • Phase 2: Governance Portal — Privacy/compliance command center adjacent to Navigator; same OMS spine, different persona defaults.
  • Enterprise expansion — Role-aware views, domain coverage growth, adoption across segments beyond initial MVP personas.

How we measure success

  • Migration — Alation content migrated via APIs; decommission by contract milestones; user training and domain champions.
  • Adoption — Weekly active users vs. ~531 Alation baseline; time-to-answer for supported consumer/analyst journeys.
  • Definition coverage — Priority domains with glossary/metric definitions reachable in Navigator.
  • Stewardship — % of in-scope assets with active steward and current attestation post-migration.
  • Boundary health — No scope creep (scrapers/MCP in Navigator); clear handoffs documented in metadata WG.
  • Design maturity — Enterprise design sign-off on IA before lock; user validation sessions completed per intake asks.
Phasing: intake + MVP scope → build on OMS + Alation migration APIs → user migration (Mar–Sep 2027) → Governance Portal phase 2.
