Overview

In-house data catalog:
requirements & migration

Replacing the external Alation catalog with Disney’s internal OMS as the system of record—preserving business metadata, aligning with enterprise standards, and executing a controlled migration before contract end.

Contract context: Alation end date October 1, 2026 — plan for parallel run or extension so cutover is data-safe, not only date-driven.

What we valued in Alation

Capability pillars the new solution must cover—through parity or an agreed substitute.

Discovery

Full-catalog search; browse datasource → schema → table → column; filters and relevance.

Business context

Glossary (terms, synonyms, links to assets); rich articles and documentation.

Trust & classification

Tags, custom fields, domains, flags, stewardship and ownership.

Lineage & pipelines

Table/column/dataflow lineage; jobs (e.g. Airflow, dbt) where connected.

Scale operations

Bulk edit, catalog sets/rules, dictionary import/export, admin analytics.

Governance

Policy center; compliance workflows; SSO and role-based access.

Validate “in use” per feature with stakeholders (feature inventory).

Slide 3 of 8

Requirements — MVP parity

Must-haves for the in-house catalog before sign-off on critical migration.

  1. Search & discovery across technical and business objects (tables, columns, glossary, docs—not only warehouse rows).
  2. Business glossary with terms, definitions, synonyms, and links to catalog assets.
  3. Asset detail showing descriptions, owners/stewards, tags, and lineage in the UI users expect.
  4. Data lineage at the level teams use today (table minimum; column/dataflow where required).
  5. Tags, domains, custom fields with bulk paths (UI + import/export).
  6. Documentation / articles with sensible information architecture (folders/collections).
  7. SSO and governance-ready auditability for sensitive metadata operations.
Phase 2+: Deeper policy workflows, analytics, AI-assisted discovery, data quality hooks—after critical parity is agreed.

Slide 4 of 8

OMS: leverage vs extend vs build

Use the platform’s strengths; be explicit about product and integration work.

  • Leverage: Large-scale technical inventory (tables, pipelines, streams, reports) and lineage entry points in the UI.
  • Extend / populate: Business fields are often placeholders—need product + integrations + stewardship, not only a one-time copy.
  • Net-new: Glossary, business-context search, bulk/rule-based governance, policy workflows—coordinate with the OMS roadmap.
Principle: Metadata-first — business context is the hardest to recreate if lost.

Slide 5 of 8

Migration — Alation → OMS

Phased approach with clear ownership for data quality at each step.

1
Inventory & extract Export glossary, articles, tags, custom fields, lineage refs, domains, flags; map to OMS.
2
Transform & load Field mapping, validation rules, reconciliation reports.
3
Parallel run Both systems available; UAT with stewards and power users.
4
Cutover Redirect users; freeze writes in Alation; OMS primary.
5
Decommission Archive; retire Alation; close contract.

Dependencies: Alation API access, OMS APIs for load, named owners per migration step.
